01 // Character Overview

Levi Ackerman stands out as an unforgettable favorite because he pairs unmatched combat mastery with a deeply compassionate heart. On the battlefield, humanity's strongest soldier moves with breathtaking speed and precision, turning impossible odds into thrilling displays of pure skill. Yet beneath his stoic, blunt exterior and quirky obsession with cleanliness lies a man who feels the weight of every lost comrade. Shaped by a harsh upbringing in the Underground city, Levi bears immense emotional burdens and impossible choices without ever losing his quiet humanity.

04 // Classified Intel & Trivia (Click to Expand)

Signature Reverse-Grip Blade Technique

Unlike standard soldiers who hold both blades forward, Levi holds his right blade backward so he can spin rapidly like a buzzsaw, maximizing cutting power while preserving momentum.

Obsession with Cleanliness & Black Tea

Having grown up in the filthy Underground district, Levi developed an intense dislike for dirt. When relaxing, he enjoys black tea and holds his teacup by the rim rather than the handle.

The Ackerman Bloodline Awakening

As a member of the Ackerman clan, Levi possesses an awakened power that grants him the physical strength and battle instincts of a Titan while remaining in human form.
